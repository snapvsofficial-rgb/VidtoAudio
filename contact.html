Frequently Asked Questions

Before sending an inquiry, check these answers to the most common questions from our users.

• Are my video or audio files ever uploaded to your servers?

No, absolutely never. VidToAudio.com operates on a 100% zero-server-upload architecture. All audio extraction and format encoding take place directly inside your device's browser using client-side WebAssembly.

• Why did a large video conversion fail or freeze?

Because conversion runs in your browser, your system's available RAM and WebAssembly allocation limit affect processing. For large files over 2 GB, ensure other heavy browser tabs are closed.

• Is VidToAudio.com free to use for personal and commercial work?

Yes, VidToAudio is 100% free with no hidden subscriptions, watermarks, or artificial limits on bitrates (supporting up to 320 kbps). It is supported by non-intrusive Google AdSense advertising.

• How do I report a bug or suggest a new codec?

Please use the form above with the subject "Technical Support" or "New Audio Format Request", and specify your browser, operating system, and the video format involved.
