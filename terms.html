02. Description of Web Service & Client-Side Operation

VidToAudio.com operates as an advanced, lightweight online web platform providing client-side media utilities:

  • Audio Track Extraction: Extracting lossless or compressed audio streams from popular video container formats (including MP4, MKV, MOV, WebM, AVI, FLV, WMV, M4V, 3GP, and MTS) into output audio codecs (including MP3, WAV, AAC, FLAC, OGG, M4A, OPUS, AIFF, and WMA).
  • Audio Transcoding: Changing audio bitrates, channels (stereo/mono), and sample rates across formats.
  • In-Browser Video Editing: Video timeline segment trimming, cutting, and volume adjustments executed without server uploads.

Pure Browser Execution: The Website does not require you to install native software, purchase licenses, or register an account. Our service executes by running standard HTML5 and WebAssembly (WASM) routines in your browser's local sandbox memory.
