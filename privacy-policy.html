Foundational Privacy Architecture: 100% Client-Side Processing

VidToAudio.com is engineered strictly as an in-browser client-side web utility utilizing WebAssembly (WASM) and compiled FFmpeg decoders. When you drop, load, convert, or edit video or audio files on our website, your media files are NEVER uploaded to, saved on, analyzed by, or stored on our servers or any remote cloud infrastructure. Zero bytes of your audio or video ever leave your computer or mobile device.

02. Zero Server Upload Architecture (Your Media Files)

Traditional media conversion websites function by transferring user files across the internet to remote servers, where the files are queued, processed, and temporarily stored. VidToAudio.com operates fundamentally differently:

  • Client-Side WebAssembly (WASM) & FFmpeg: When you open our website, your browser loads a lightweight WebAssembly binary compiled from the open-source FFmpeg multimedia framework. All file decoding, audio demuxing, sample rate conversions, and encoding take place exclusively inside your device's browser execution sandbox.
  • Zero Server Uploads: When you select or drag a video or audio file into VidToAudio.com, the file is read into your browser's local memory (RAM) via standard HTML5 File APIs. Not a single packet or byte of your media is ever uploaded to our web servers, cloud databases, or third-party servers.
  • No Server Storage, Inspection, or Logging: Because your media never touches our servers, it is technically impossible for us or any third party to view, copy, log, analyze, or leak your personal video recordings, family memories, podcasts, or confidential business media.
  • Automatic Memory Cleanup: The temporary working data allocated during conversion remains strictly in your device's RAM. As soon as you download your converted audio file, reload the page, or close the browser tab, all allocated memory buffers are immediately and permanently cleared by your operating system.
